# Privacy Policy — Steal An Egg Notifier

**Last updated: October 27, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Steal An Egg Notifier ("the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") collects, processes, stores, and uses information when operating on Discord.

Steal An Egg Notifier provides notifications for rare egg and pet spawns in the Roblox game Steal An Egg, alongside optional server management, moderation, and community features.

By using the Bot or participating in Discord servers where its features are enabled, your information may be processed as described in this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features enabled, the Bot may process or store the following information:

- **Discord identifiers:** User IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and message IDs.
- **Member information:** Usernames, display names, assigned roles, join and leave events, and relevant timestamps.
- **Message content:** Messages processed for spam detection, scam prevention, moderation, counting, and egg spawn notifications.
- **Moderation records:** Warning reasons, message excerpts, timestamps, user IDs, and moderation actions.
- **Community statistics:** Invite counts, counting scores, experience points, leveling progress, giveaway participation, and member statistics.
- **Support tickets:** Ticket identifiers, participants, status information, and relevant messages or records where retained.
- **Server settings:** Notification configurations, selected roles, channel settings, and feature preferences.

We do not require your Discord password or payment card information.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

- Deliver Divine, Eternal, and Secret Egg spawn notifications.
- Provide Premium notification features and manage notification roles.
- Process incoming game spawn information.
- Detect spam, suspicious links, scams, excessive mentions, and potentially unsafe attachments.
- Issue warnings, apply moderation actions, and maintain moderation history.
- Operate counting, leveling, giveaways, invite tracking, and other community features.
- Handle support tickets and assist server staff.
- Maintain server configurations and functionality after Bot restarts.
- Investigate technical issues, prevent abuse, and maintain Bot security.

Information is processed only for purposes related to operating, maintaining, and securing the Bot and its features.

## 4. Message Content and Moderation

The Bot may automatically inspect messages in channels where relevant features are enabled.

This includes checking for prohibited links, spam, suspicious content, excessive mentions, and other potential rule violations.

When a moderation action occurs, the Bot may store part of the original message alongside the warning reason, relevant Discord identifiers, and timestamps.

Message content may also be processed to verify counting-game entries or extract information from game spawn notifications.

Not every message processed by the Bot is permanently stored. Some information is processed temporarily in memory.

The Bot does not currently offer an individual opt-out from automatic moderation checks in participating channels.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

The Bot stores certain operational information in persistent JSON files on its hosting infrastructure.

This may include Discord identifiers, server configurations, moderation records, invite statistics, counting data, leveling information, and ticket-related records.

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the relevant features, maintain operational records, investigate moderation incidents, or address security concerns.

Some records do not currently have a fixed automatic deletion period and may remain stored until manually removed, no longer required, or deleted following an applicable data request.

We review requests to delete information and aim to avoid retaining personal data longer than necessary.

## 6. Premium Memberships and Patreon

Steal An Egg Notifier offers optional Premium features.

Premium subscriptions and payments are managed through Patreon. Patreon may assign a Premium role to a connected Discord account through its Discord integration.

The Bot checks Discord roles to determine whether a member is eligible to access Premium features and notification roles.

The Bot does not directly collect or store payment card information.

Patreon's handling of payment and subscription information is governed by its own privacy policy.

## 7. Third-Party Services

The Bot relies on third-party services to provide its functionality, including:

- **Discord:** For messages, interactions, roles, and server functionality.
- **Hosting infrastructure:** For running the Bot and storing operational data.
- **Patreon:** For optional Premium subscriptions and Discord membership integration.
- **Roblox:** For links to the supported game.

These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies.

Information may be accessible to the Bot operator, necessary hosting services, or authorized server staff where required to operate the Bot, review moderation incidents, or manage support requests.
